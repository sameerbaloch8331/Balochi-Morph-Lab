Word Forge

Build English words from their morphemes. Clear three levels to win.

How to play

Read the meaning, then tap the tiles in the right order to build the word. Each level has 6 words and you get 3 lives per level. Finish all 6 words with a life left to move up.

prefix comes before the root, root carries the core meaning, suffix comes after it.
